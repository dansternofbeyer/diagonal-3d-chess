    ..HOW TO PLAY..

    MOVING AROUND

    • Spin: drag with the left mouse button (one finger on a phone).
    • Pan: drag with the middle mouse button (hold the wheel down) or the right button, or shift-drag (two fingers on a phone).
    • Zoom: scroll wheel or pinch.
    • Options: “Hide options”, top right, gives the board the whole window. “Reset view” goes back to the starting view. Click a box's heading to open or close it.

    MOVING PIECES

    • Click a piece. Cubes, dots or cube edges (your choice, in Show moves as) show where it can go. Orange ones are captures.
    • Click a highlighted cube to move there. Click empty space or press Esc to clear.
    • Castling works as in 2D chess, along the home edge: select the king and the castling moves light up, two cubes toward a rook. It is only offered if neither piece has moved, the cubes between them are empty, and the king is not in check, crossing an attacked cube or landing on one.
    • Undo last move (in the Moves box, or Ctrl+Z) takes back the last move.

    CLASSIC OR FREE

    Classic moves shows each piece's normal moves. Free moves lets any piece go anywhere: pick a plane with the slider and every cube in it lights up. This is the "incorrect move" from the original: the board does not decide the rules, the players do.

    A piece can always be taken off the board by hand: select it and press Delete.

    THE BOARD

    The board is an 8×8×8 cube, tilted 45° so one edge points at each player. Red starts on the front edge, Black on the back edge. Each upright slice of the cube is a plane, numbered 0 (Red's edge) to 14 (Black's edge).

    TAKEN PIECES

    Taken pieces pop up on the rose rail, beside the player who took them.

    SAVE AND LOAD

    Under the GAME menu, Save game downloads a .txt file listing every piece. It is plain text, so you can edit it to set up puzzles and send it to a friend. Load game (or drop the file on the board) sets the pieces up again.

    The game in progress is also kept in this browser, so visiting the Studio or reloading the page picks up where you left off. New game starts over.

    EXPLORE MOVES

    Turn Explore moves ON and click several pieces, from either side, to see all their moves at once: red for Red, white for Black, purple where both sides can reach. Click a piece again to drop it. Nothing moves while it is on; turn it OFF to play again. Good for seeing who guards what.

    MOVE LINES

    Tick Move lines to draw a thin line along each move's path, through every cube it passes.

    ..PHILOSOPHY..
    3D CHESSDESIGN : DAN BEYER

    Games evolve. It is in their nature to change. As physical games become digitized, their ability to evolve is constricted by the hard coding of the "correct move".

    This is why Diagonal 3D Chess can make "incorrect" moves. It is less a 3D chess game than a 3D chess board: a tool for many variations of 3D chess.

    OPEN QUESTIONS FOR PLAYERS

    • A pawn crossing one plane at a time takes about 8 moves to reach the middle and 14 to reach the far edge. When should it promote?
    • A pawn fills a whole row, so it can be taken at several spots, and can take several pieces at once. Is that OK?

    This version uses one answer to each (promote at the far edge; yes, take them all), but Free moves and hand-edited save files let you play by your own.

    HISTORY

    Made in 2006 as a senior thesis: Maya models in Director 3D, programmed in Lingo. Rebuilt in 2026 for the browser with three.js, and models made in Blender. Original site: thehinge.net/3dchess.
